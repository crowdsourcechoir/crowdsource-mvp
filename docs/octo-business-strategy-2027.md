# Crowdsource Choir

2027–2028 | Internal Business Strategy

A Participatory Entertainment Company

This is the commercial source of truth for OCTO. Product, sales, and show work
should stay coherent with it. The living-system map is in
[`octo-living-system-workspace.md`](./octo-living-system-workspace.md). The
October 2026 sales workload mix still lives in `lib/sales/strategy/playbook.ts`
until those targets are replaced on purpose.

Crowdsource Choir accelerates and amplifies collective creativity—turning
audiences into creators and cultivating the conditions for collective
effervescence to emerge. We source the potential of people, purpose, and place,
then bring it to life through participatory entertainment and artistic blooms.

## The opportunity

Build a lean, profitable creative company whose premium engagements generate
cash flow while partnerships help finance owned entertainment properties,
commissioned activations, and reusable assets. Scale through distribution
partners, repeatable formats, sponsorships, and long-term relationships—not
simply more founder-led performances.

## The strategic frame

Culture → Ecosystem → Crowdsource Choir → People • Purpose • Value

Across conferences, sports, brands, schools, districts, venues, destinations,
and communities, participation becomes shared creative expression.

Community authorship, attribution, recognition, and fair value exchange are
fundamental to the model.

## Two complementary business models

These two models share creative methods, technology, musicians, production
systems, audience participation expertise, and partner relationships.

### 1. Premium commissioned experiences

Organizations commission Crowdsource Choir to create participatory experiences
for their people, purpose, and place.

| Offering | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Anthem | Premium, original, large-scale participatory music experiences. |
| Fan Chant | Original fan rituals and activations for sports organizations. |
| Song Garden | Commissioned community and place-based creative activations. |
| Song Garden Journeys (2028+) | Premium, personally guided, place-based creative retreats. |

Business model: engagement fees, creative commissions, production fees,
multi-event programs, and recurring institutional partnerships.

### 2. Owned entertainment properties

Crowdsource Choir develops, owns, and distributes entertainment formats that
audiences, venues, sponsors, and presenting partners can support.

| Offering | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Rave Choir | Flagship multigenerational dance-and-sing entertainment property. |
| Holiday Rave Choir | Seasonal edition of Rave Choir, for families, venues, and holiday audiences. |
| A Thriller Night | Halloween-themed participatory music experience with its own creative identity. |
| Other themed shows | Seasonal, cultural, and special-event formats developed as opportunities justify investment. |

Business model: sponsorships, presenting partnerships, commissioned bookings,
ticketing, festivals, residencies, and eventually licensing and touring.

Commercial principle: secure committed funding and distribution before taking
on significant production risk. Ticket sales provide upside but should not be
the sole foundation for financing a show.

## Four connected creative engines

### Anthem — primary cash engine

Premium, commissioned, large-scale original anthem experiences. The current
commercial lead and primary source of operating revenue.

- Revenue: engagement fees; higher-value conference, fundraising, sports, and brand commissions; media enhancements; repeat engagements.
- Assets built: flagship film and case studies; original anthem repertoire; proof of scale; client credibility; repeat relationships; creative methodology.
- Critical partners: event planners, speaker bureaus, experiential agencies, production partners, conference organizers, and institutional buyers.
- 12–24 month focus: turn the 2,000-person performance into compelling proof of scale. Pursue larger $25K–$50K+ opportunities, expand partner-led bookings, increase repeat business, and reduce the amount of direct sales required for each engagement.

### Rave Choir — owned entertainment and IP engine

A multigenerational dance-and-sing property that Crowdsource Choir produces and
owns. The flagship format can expand into seasonal editions, special
productions, venue residencies, and eventually additional markets.

Entertainment portfolio:

- Rave Choir — flagship dance-and-sing experience.
- Holiday Rave Choir — seasonal, family-friendly edition.
- A Thriller Night — distinct Halloween-themed show, with potential to develop as a recurring property.
- Other themed shows — developed selectively based on demand, partnerships, and commercial viability.

- Revenue: presenting sponsorships; ticketing; venue partnerships; commissioned shows; festivals; brand activations; future touring and licensing.
- Assets built: owned entertainment IP; recognizable show formats; audience relationships; sponsor inventory; production playbooks; recurring event series; media and promotional assets.
- Critical partners: a committed sponsor or underwriting; a venue partner; a promoter or marketing and distribution partner; producer and operational support.
- 12–24 month focus: establish one financially viable, funded Seattle Rave Choir flagship or series. Develop themed editions where existing partnerships and audience demand support them.
- Commercial requirement: no speculative ticketed productions without a credible funding, promotion, and break-even plan. Sponsorship may fund marketing, but a capable promotional partner must still be accountable for audience development.

### Fan Chant — sports growth engine

Original chants, rituals, and participatory fan experiences that turn
spectators into active contributors to team culture.

- Revenue: team and league commissions; sponsor-backed activations; halftime experiences; seasonal partnerships; recurring fan programs.
- Assets built: original chant IP; fan participation formats; team relationships; reusable activation playbooks; sports-specific case studies.
- Critical partners: an anchor team or league; game presentation and marketing partners; potential sponsors; distribution through sports organizations.
- 12–24 month focus: secure one or two paid pilots, document outcomes, and test a repeatable seasonal partnership model.
- Growth principle: develop Fan Chant in response to funded opportunities rather than independently financing a speculative sports property.

### Song Garden — commissioned creative activation engine

An intentional, bounded creative garden for a school, district, community,
institution, or destination.

Community contributions become creative source material that blooms into an
Anthem, public installation, film, performance, or multiple artistic
expressions developed by different artists.

The garden is not simply a content collection tool. Its purpose is to cultivate
creative expression and shared experiences.

- Revenue: commissioned gardens; multi-event civic and district programs; creative direction and activation fees; associated Anthem and artist commissions; recurring institutional programs.
- Assets built: participation methodology; reusable digital and physical tools; appropriately permissioned creative source libraries; artist networks; repeatable activation formats; community and institutional relationships.
- Critical partners: commissioning clients; trusted artist collaborators; delivery partners; consent, attribution, and rights systems; reusable activation infrastructure.
- 12–24 month focus: deliver a small number of high-impact commissioned gardens, demonstrate the potential for multiple artistic blooms, and improve reusable infrastructure.
- Future opportunity — Song Garden Journeys (2028+): premium, approximately weeklong, place-based creative retreats with a small group of participants. Potential partnership with Inside Story, combining destination experiences with personally guided collective creativity. Revenue could come from premium participant fees, creative direction fees, and partnership revenue sharing. The initial model would be founder-guided, with hospitality and travel operations handled by a specialist partner.
- Self-service Song Gardens remain a longer-term possibility, not a current commercial priority.

## How the business works together

This is a reinforcing business system, not a required sequence. Each offering
can operate independently while contributing to the larger company.

1. Anthem generates cash flow and commercial credibility. Premium engagements create the financial foundation while producing proof of scale, original music, content, and access to institutional buyers.
2. Song Garden cultivates creative potential. Its participation methodology can support Anthem, Fan Chant, Rave Choir, and independent artistic blooms. Commissioned gardens also generate revenue in their own right.
3. Owned entertainment properties create repeatable experiences. Rave Choir and themed shows build audience relationships, sponsor opportunities, recognizable formats, and intellectual property that extends beyond individual commissions.
4. Fan Chant expands the model into sports culture. Sports partnerships create opportunities for recurring fan experiences, original chants, sponsorships, and potentially repeatable team programs.
5. Capture and distribution amplify the value. Films, original music, case studies, community stories, and promotional content make each successful experience visible to future clients, partners, and audiences.
6. Partnerships generate the next opportunities. Agencies, promoters, venues, sponsors, sports organizations, institutions, and destination partners help finance, distribute, and expand the experiences.
7. The portfolio compounds in value. Each successful project contributes to stronger recognition, owned assets, reusable methods, and repeat business.

## The partnership strategy

Crowdsource Choir should remain lean at its center and expansive through partnerships.

| Partners | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Distribution | Event planners, experiential agencies, speaker bureaus, promoters, sports organizations, and venue networks bring opportunities and audiences. |
| Funding | Sponsors, institutions, brands, civic organizations, and commissioning clients finance experiences and recurring programming. |
| Creative | Musicians, visual artists, filmmakers, producers, and facilitators expand the forms collective creativity can take. |
| Production | Venues, event producers, technical providers, and hospitality operators support delivery without requiring Crowdsource Choir to build a large permanent organization. |

The strategic goal: more revenue and creative reach per unit of founder time,
supported by profitable engagements, repeatable formats, and strong partnerships.

## Decision filter

Evaluate every opportunity through three criteria:

1. Creative potential. Does this opportunity offer meaningful possibilities for collective creativity, participation, artistic expression, and collective effervescence?
2. Financial viability. Are the economics sound? Can the opportunity generate meaningful profit or contribute to a sustainable revenue model?
3. Quality of partnership. Are the people and organizations involved aligned, capable, collaborative, and committed to mutual success?

Prioritize opportunities where all three are strong. Consider opportunities with
two strong dimensions when there is a clear reason to invest, but avoid
routinely sacrificing financial viability or partnership quality for creative
excitement.

Asset creation, reusable IP, and repeat business remain important business
outcomes, but they do not replace these three criteria.

## North star

Coherence is the core. Flow is the movement. Flourishing is the outcome.

Crowdsource Choir is building a participatory entertainment company that earns
through premium creative experiences, grows through partnerships, and compounds
value through owned entertainment properties, repeatable formats, and
commissioned creative activations.

The goal is not simply to produce more events. It is to create the conditions
for more collective creativity to bloom—while building a financially flourishing
company capable of reaching more people, places, and communities.
