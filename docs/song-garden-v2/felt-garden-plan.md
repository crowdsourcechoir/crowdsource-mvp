# One chant — a plan for a felt garden

Status: **proposal** (no build in this pass)
Fits under: Song Garden V2 (`WorldJourney`), persistent garden (`/g/[slug]`), Roots loop
Related: [`architecture.md`](./architecture.md), [`persistent-world-spec.md`](./persistent-world-spec.md), `docs/octo-living-system-workspace.md`

The whole visit is one question.

**What’s your chant?**

---

## Why one prompt

A chant is already the game piece. It is short. It is meant to be said out loud. It is meant to sit beside other people’s chants. Everyone who opens the garden is answering the same thing, so the field is one bed, and a new plant is obviously a new chant.

The journey today asks for several answers, celebrates each for about a second, and moves on. More questions would add more buckets. One question lets the time after planting become the experience: you stay in the field, your flower is there, and other chants arrive in the soil while you watch.

That also answers the typing problem. “What’s your chant?” is a line you sing or shout. Typing is a way to leave the words when you will not use your voice. It is the same seed, with a quieter plant. The prompt itself leans toward a voice.

Ballard’s garden already asks a version of this in two zones (“What’s your chant idea for the next game?”) and then offers a text box. This plan is that question as the entire world, with the voice planted in the ground.

---

## The aim

You give a chant before you hear anyone else’s. You watch it take root. Then the field opens and the crowd is actually there: anonymous flowers you can touch and hear. You can leave. When you come back, your chant is where you left it, and the field has filled in around it.

The score is the field.

---

## Why it feels like a bucket today

What a participant gets when they submit:

1. A one-second ring and sparkle (`CelebrationBurst`), then it is gone.
2. A small glowing dot (`WorldGrowthLayer`). A typed line and a sung line are the same kind of dot.
3. Other people appear as a pill of text (`WorldPresenceTicker`): “Someone just added a sound.”
4. The card moves on, or the visit ends on a thank-you line.

The world underneath can already shift, store shared energy, and remember marks. Those systems change numbers. They do not yet perform a planting, and they do not yet let you stay with what you planted.

People type because Type, Record, Video, and Photo are equal circles, and Record is a long capture with a review step. Nothing in the garden looks or sounds better if you chanted.

---

## Principles

1. **One bed.** One question, one field. Interaction after the plant is how the visit gets richer. New questions are how it turns back into a form.
2. **The reward is a change you can point at.** Your flower opens. The light stays warmer. Tomorrow there are more flowers. Points, badges, streaks, and leaderboards stay out.
3. **The field is sealed until your chant is in the ground.** Before that, you may see that the bed is alive. You may not read or hear a chant.
4. **After you plant, a chant can be heard in full.** It is one phrase. Hiding it behind a blur would hide the crowd. What stays hidden is the person: no name, no face, no account. The newest chant waits, so the voice beside you in the room is not the first one you hear.
5. **A voice grows a flower. A typed line grows a leaf.** Both are chants. The flower can be played. The leaf can be read. Yours is always fully yours. The shared field hears flowers and shows leaves only after you have planted.
6. **The garden collects. The Bloom sings it back.** Composer chooses what the room actually chants. The field is a place of seeds, not an open mic and not a feed.

## A visit

You open the link. The field is already planted with closed buds. They sway. Now and then the soil lifts where a new one is forming. There is no lyric in the air, and no melody you could steal. You know the chant exists. You do not know it yet.

One line sits over the field: What’s your chant?

The large circle says Chant. You hold it and give a phrase, a few seconds. The ring moves with your volume. Under the circle, small: or write it.

You let go. The question gets out of the way. A seed drops into a spot that stays yours. The soil opens, a stem rises, a flower opens. Your chant plays once from the flower. The phone pulses. The whole field warms and stays warm.

The buds around you open. You tap one. Someone else’s chant plays, a whole phrase, with no name on it. You tap a leaf and read a line. You wander. While you stand there, a new bud breaks the soil at the edge of the field. If you tap it after a short wait, there is another chant. You were not told about it in a caption. You saw it grow.

You leave. The next day the same phone opens the same field. Your flower is obvious. New ones have grown since you left. You can chant again only as another seed, a second plant, still inside this same question. The first one does not move.

## What you can do, and when

| Moment | What you can do | What you cannot do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arrive | Watch closed buds. Feel new ones push up. | Hear or read any chant |
| Offer | Hold Chant, or write one line | Browse the field for ideas |
| Plant | Watch your seed become a flower or a leaf. Hear or read your own chant | — |
| Stay | Tap other plants. Hear full chants. Read lines. Watch new ones grow in | See who made them. Hear the one that just landed beside you |
| Return | Find yours. Notice what grew. Plant another chant if you want | Start over as if the field forgot you |

A photo or a video can wait. This question wants a voice or a line.

## The plant

Same motion every time, about two seconds, then it stays:

| Beat | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Release | The chant leaves the circle and falls to your spot |
| Soil | The mound opens |
| Rise | A stem comes up |
| Open | A flower opens, or a leaf unfurls |
| Stay | It sways there for the rest of the visit and on return |

A chant you sang or shouted becomes the flower. Tap it and it plays. A chant you typed becomes the leaf. Tap it and the line is there. Build this as motion stages on the growth-node position. Vine lines that draw themselves and stop already failed that test. A plant has to arrive, then keep living.

Your plant is brighter. When the shared garden version changes, someone else’s plant plays the smaller version of the same growth, with a soft haptic. The sparkle can glint at the moment the seed hits. The plant is the celebration.

Before you have chanted, those other plants remain closed buds. The growth is visible. The phrase is not.

## The seal

**Before your chant**

- Density is the crowd. A full field looks full. A quiet one looks bare.
- A new contribution is soil moving and a bud swelling.
- No words, no recordings, no melodies, no names.

**After your chant**

- Your plant plays or shows your own phrase immediately.
- Other plants open onto real chants: the phrase, the whole short take.
- They are shuffled and held back by a few minutes.
- Nothing on the plant identifies a person.

**On a later visit**

- The field opens at once, because this device already planted.
- A shared phone is already unlocked after the first person. That is acceptable for the first version. “Plant another” adds a seed. It does not hide the field again.

The public garden (`/g/[slug]`) uses the same seal. Someone who has never chanted sees a living field of closed buds. Someone who has, sees the chants.

## Voice, with a door for words

- The primary control is the circle: Chant.
- One phrase, about six to eight seconds, with a clear stop. One confirmation: Plant it.
- The ring follows the microphone, which it already can in `VoiceMomentPad`. That is the play inside the recording.
- Hearing yourself happens on the flower, so review is the reward.
- “Or write it” is a single line under the circle. It plants a leaf. It is there for the person who will not sing, and for a noisy room.
- A flower moves the shared vocal layer more than a leaf moves the text layer. The air of the field is thicker when people chant. Other people still hear nothing intelligible until they have planted their own.

Name and email, if a garden truly needs them, sit outside this moment. They are not the chant.

---

## Coming back

There is no thank-you card and no next question. After the plant, you are in the field. That is the end of the first visit and the start of every later one.

- The same link, on the same phone, restores your flower. Device marks already exist for this.
- Growth since last visit is the first thing you notice. New plants ease in. Yours is simply there.
- Between Blooms, `/g/[slug]` is this field, not a map whose only life is a text box.
- Later, Composer can turn these seeds into the chant the Bloom actually sings, and that recording can play over the field. Your flower is the proof you are in it. That waits until the plant, the seal, and the return are real.

---

## What stays out

- A second creative question in the first version.
- Points, XP, badges, streaks, and public ranks.
- Names, faces, and avatars on plants.
- Any chant you can hear or read before you have given one.
- The newest chant offered first to the person who just planted.
- A mascot, or vine-lines that draw and stop.
- A scrolling feed of clips. You move through a field and tap what grew.
- Camera capture, until the chant itself feels alive.

## How to start

Two phones, one garden, one question. One plants. The other sees a bud rise and cannot hear the phrase. After the second phone plants, it can.

### First build — plant and stay

- The screen is the field plus “What’s your chant?”
- Chant, or write one line.
- Staged growth. Your plant stays. The question does not advance.
- Other people’s new plants grow in as closed buds, with a soft haptic.
- No public audio or text yet.

This uses `WorldGrowthLayer`, `useCelebration`, `pulseHaptic`, and the snapshot poll.

### Second build — open the field

- After you plant, buds open into anonymous chants.
- Tap a flower to hear the phrase. Tap a leaf to read the line.
- Delay and shuffle, so the newest neighbor is not first.
- One server rule for what a sealed field may return, shared by the journey and `/g/[slug]`.

### Third build — the return

- Reopen the link and land in your field, already unsealed.
- Show what grew since last time.
- Offer “Plant another” as a second seed on the same question.

The recording of the crowd chant, sung back at a Bloom, comes after those three.

Events that still need a longer journey can keep `WorldJourney` as it is. The first garden that should feel alive is the one whose only prompt is the chant.

## Success, in the room

Someone who will not sing can write one line and see a leaf that is theirs.

Someone who chants gets a flower and can hear themselves in it.

A second phone sees that flower come up as a closed bud, and learns the phrase only after giving one of their own.

Someone who came yesterday can tell the field is not where they left it.
