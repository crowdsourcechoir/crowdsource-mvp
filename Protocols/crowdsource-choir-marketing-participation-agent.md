Crowdsource Choir Marketing + Participation Agent (v0.1)

This document defines the first shared core for the Crowdsource Choir Brand Brain.

The system is not generic automated marketing.

It is the operating system for participatory cultural creation.

Core Purpose

Build a centralized AI-assisted creative operations system that:

- automates repetitive marketing workflows
- supports ticket sales and private-event sales
- integrates audience participation into marketing
- transforms marketing into pre-event engagement
- creates scalable systems for participatory cultural experiences
- frees Joel to focus on music craft, immersive show development, leadership, artistic direction, relationships, and partnerships

Core Strategic Insight

Marketing becomes participation.

The old pattern:

Ad -> Ticket -> Attend

The Crowdsource Choir pattern:

Signal -> Contribution -> Belonging -> Anticipation -> Participation -> Artifact -> Ongoing Culture

Audience members should not simply consume promotion.
They begin co-creating the experience before arriving.

Song Garden Logic

The Song Garden is not content submission.

It is participatory cultural contribution.

Contribution prompts should:

- be emotionally specific
- ask for small, low-friction responses
- explain how contributions may become music, visuals, or shared story
- preserve Joel's artistic direction
- make participants feel that anticipation has already begun

Moderation principles:

- screen for safety, consent, and relevance
- avoid sensitive personal data
- privilege vivid, usable, emotionally resonant material
- keep creative authority with the artist
