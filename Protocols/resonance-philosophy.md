# Resonance philosophy

Crowdsource Choir Signal is a collective sensing interface for participatory
music. Participants are not choosing what they like; they are sensing what
resonates in the body.

The participant loop is:

```text
Listen -> Feel -> Hold -> Release -> Signal -> Emergence
```

The interface should move people from cognition into presence, emotion,
embodiment, listening, and collective coherence. It should feel closer to an
immersive musical instrument, responsive environment, ritual interface, or living
signal system than a form, game show, polling platform, survey, or voting app.

Participant-facing language should remain minimal. Do not use vote, poll,
submit, option, winner, favorite, ranking, survey, or preference language in the
participant experience.

## Product implications

- Each color field represents a felt resonance field, not a choice.
- Holding the orb is a continuous signal of embodied resonance, not a selection.
- The participant surface should stay simple enough to use with eyes closed after
  orientation.
- Admin controls should support live alignment between stage color, room sound,
  and participant phone color.
- Complexity belongs under the surface; the emotional insight is the innovation.
